mzQC Viewer
===========

The mzQC Viewer shows mzQC files in your browser: the highlighted, foldable
file content next to a rendered report with tables and plots.
Files you open are read by your browser only; nothing is uploaded.

narrower ones it switches between them with tabs. Open it in its own tab for
more room.
